Privacy Policy

Watershed · Last updated October 22, 2026

Watershed is a personal, single-user application. Its only user is its owner and developer. It has no customers, no accounts, and no public access. This policy exists to describe, plainly, what the application does with data.

What data it accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google's OAuth flow, Watershed accesses data in the owner's own Google account via Google APIs, including Gmail. It accesses no one else's data.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used solely for the owner's personal purposes. It is processed and stored on hardware the owner physically controls, retained at the owner's discretion, and can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. No advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind is involved. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account third-party access page, which immediately invalidates the application's tokens.

Contact

Questions can be directed to the owner via github.com/rodly.
